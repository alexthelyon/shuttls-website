Questions

Good to know.

How do I track payments for my badminton club?

With Shuttls, every player who checks in appears on your roster with what they owe and whether they have paid by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, cash or bank transfer. You see it live, so there is no end-of-night reconciling.

Can I sell memberships to badminton club members?

Yes. Create weekly, monthly or yearly plans with a set number of sessions or unlimited sessions, or a one-time term membership with a custom start and end date. Players join in a tap from check-in.

What does it cost to take card payments?

Shuttls itself is free to run a club. When you take card payments, the payment provider's normal card fees apply.

Can players still pay by cash?

Yes. Players pick pay later or cash and you mark it off on the roster, so cash payers are tracked alongside everyone else.
