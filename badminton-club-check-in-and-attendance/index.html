Questions

Good to know.

How do I track attendance at a badminton club?

Print your Shuttls QR poster and have players scan it as they arrive. Every check-in is recorded against the session, giving you a live roster and a full attendance history without any manual entry.

Can I stop people checking in from home?

Yes. Turn off remote check-in from the app and players have to scan the QR at the venue.

What happens if my session is full?

Set spots per session. When it fills, players see it is full instead of turning up to a packed hall, and you can still add people at the door.

Does it work on Android and iPhone?

Yes. Shuttls works on iPhone and Android, plus Apple Watch, Wear OS, Garmin and Amazfit watches.
