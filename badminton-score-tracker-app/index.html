Questions

Good to know.

What is the best app to track badminton scores?

Shuttls scores singles and doubles from your phone or your watch, logs every match automatically and tracks your grade, form and partners over time. It is free on iPhone and Android.

Can I score badminton on my smartwatch?

Yes. Shuttls works on Apple Watch, Wear OS, Garmin and Amazfit watches, and a match scored on your wrist appears on your phone.

Can I use Shuttls without a club?

Yes. Make a private group, invite your friends and play together. Every game is logged with rankings for the group.

How does my badminton grade work?

Everyone starts on a 3.00 grade. It moves as you win and lose matches in your club or group, and you can join a division and climb the ladder.
