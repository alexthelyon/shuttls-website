Club night

Fill every court
in one tap.

See every court at a glance. Tap Generate and Shuttls fills every free court: the next players are picked by games played and time waiting, matched by level, and split into balanced teams.

  • Matched by level: players with a similar club level share a court, with teams balanced strongest and weakest against the middle two.
  • Everyone gets a game: fewest games and longest wait go first, so nobody is left standing around.
  • Every result counts: scores update club levels, player stats and your leaderboard. Prefer to pick yourself? Place players by hand.

Questions

Good to know.

Do my players need the app?

Yes, check-in lives in Shuttls so it can track attendance and payments. Scanning your QR on a phone without the app opens a page that links straight to the App Store. After they install and sign up, they’re taken to your club’s check-in.

Is Shuttls free?

Players can download Shuttls free on the App Store. If you take card payments, the payment provider’s normal card fees apply.

How do I get paid?

Connect your club’s payment account in the app. Players can pay with Apple Pay or card at check-in, or take out a membership. Prefer cash? Players pick “pay later or cash” and you mark it off on the roster.

What if my club is full?

Set spots per session and an overall club size. When a session fills, players see it’s full instead of turning up to a packed hall, and you can still add people at the door.

Can I keep check-in to people actually at the venue?

Yes. Turn off check-in from the app and players have to scan the QR at the door.

Is there an Android app?

Shuttls is on iPhone and Apple Watch today. An Android app is on the way. Get in touch below and we’ll let you know.
