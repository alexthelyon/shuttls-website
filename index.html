Questions

Good to know.

Do my players need the app?

Yes — check-in lives in Shuttls so it can track attendance and payments. Scanning your QR on a phone without the app opens a page that links straight to the App Store. After they install and sign up, they’re taken to your club’s check-in.

Is Shuttls free?

Players can download Shuttls free on the App Store. If you take online payments, they’re processed by Stripe, whose card fees apply.

How do I get paid?

Connect a Stripe account in the club’s settings. Players can pay with Apple Pay or card at check-in, or take out a membership plan. Prefer cash? Players pick “pay later or cash” and you mark it off on the roster.

What if my club is full?

Set spots per session and an overall club size. When a session fills, players see it’s full instead of turning up to a packed hall — and you can still add people at the door.

Can I keep check-in to people actually at the venue?

Yes. Turn off tap-to-check-in and players have to scan the QR at the door.

Is there an Android app?

Shuttls is on iPhone and Apple Watch today. An Android app is on the way — get in touch below and we’ll let you know.
