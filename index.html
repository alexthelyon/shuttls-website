Live Match Tracking

Track every point.
Effortlessly.

Shuttls records your matches in real time — no paper, no guessing. Just tap to update the score and let the app handle everything else. Singles, doubles, best-of-three: all supported.

  • Tap-to-score interface, instant updates
  • Singles and doubles match modes
  • Match history synced to the cloud
  • XP earned automatically after each game
Advanced Analytics

Stats that actually
mean something.

Every match you play feeds your personal analytics engine. Track your win rate over time, see how your XP grows, and get heart rate and calorie data synced from Apple Watch — ready in seconds, every time.

  • Win/loss tracking across all matches
  • XP progression and milestone tracking
  • Heart rate and calorie burn (via Apple Watch)
  • Historical data visualisation
