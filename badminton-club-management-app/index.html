What you get

Badminton club management, done properly.

  • Create your club in minutes: Name your club, add your venue and a weekly schedule. Sessions are created ahead of time and topped up automatically.
  • Scan-to-check-in: Print your club's QR poster. Players scan it on arrival, join the roster and pay or use their membership in one flow.

Questions

Good to know.

What is the best way to manage a badminton club?

Use one app that handles sign-in, attendance and payments together. Shuttls lets players scan a QR code to check in, shows you a live roster with who has paid, and keeps a record of every session, so there is nothing to collate afterwards.

Is Shuttls free for badminton clubs?

Yes. Creating and running a club on Shuttls is free. If you take card payments, the payment provider's normal card fees apply. Shuttls+ For Clubs is an optional subscription that adds extras such as session recaps, deeper insights and more admins.

Do my players need to download the app?

Yes, check-in lives in Shuttls so it can track attendance and payments. If a player scans your QR without the app, they land on a page that links straight to the App Store or Google Play, then continue to your club's check-in after signing up.

Can more than one person help run the club?

Yes. You can add admins to help run sessions. Shuttls+ For Clubs lets you add more than three.
